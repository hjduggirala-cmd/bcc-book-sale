Every spring, hundreds of volunteers collect, sort, and organize an incredible collection of donated books, transforming much of B-CC's first floor into a two-day treasure hunt for book lovers of all ages.

Where your money goes

100% of proceeds support B-CC students and programs through the PTSA — teacher grants and classroom supplies, teacher appreciation events, the BCC Food Pantry, and student activities. Thank you for supporting our school community.
