A Sale Favorite

Blind Date with a Book

Every year our volunteers wrap books in kraft paper and hand-draw the covers. No title. No author. Just three hints about what's inside — and you don't find out until you unwrap it.

Every cover is drawn by hand by a student volunteer. They go fast — come early.

Worth a Look

Theme Tables

Beyond the 59 categories, our volunteers build displays that change every year — BookTok, Read It Before the Movie, and the Banned Books table, which has become one of the most talked-about corners of the sale.

Search our Banned Book Guide →

Students

Earn SSL Hours

Montgomery County students can earn Student Service Learning hours at every drop-off, sort day, set-up shift, and sale day — and volunteers get to take home a book of their choosing.

No experience needed. Bring a friend — most people do.

Ways to Help

  • Drop-off days — help unload and receive donated books
  • Sort days — sort donations into categories. Times vary by date, so email us to confirm. Enter through the side door near the dumpsters.
  • Set-up week — transform the school into a bookstore
  • Sale days — cashiering, restocking, and helping shoppers
  • Cleanup — pack up after the sale wraps

Parking & Getting Here

Recommended: county public garage

7400 Waverly Street — $1.75/hour Saturday, free on Sunday. About a five-minute walk to B-CC.

Metro & bus

Bethesda Metro (Red Line), 5–7 minute walk. WMATA buses M70 & M22; Ride On routes 29, 30, 32, 34, 36, 47, and 70 to Bethesda Metro.

Walk or bike

Bike racks are available at the school.

School parking is extremely limited. Please respect our neighbors and follow all no-parking and reserved-parking signs.

Every spring, hundreds of volunteers collect, sort, and organize an incredible collection of donated books, transforming much of B-CC's first floor into a two-day treasure hunt for book lovers of all ages.

Where your money goes

100% of proceeds support B-CC students and programs through the PTSA — teacher grants and classroom supplies, teacher appreciation events, the BCC Food Pantry, and student activities. Thank you for supporting our school community.
